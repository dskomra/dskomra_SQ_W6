# Side Quest Week 6

## Setup and Interaction Instructions

**Running the Sketch**

Right-click on index.html and open in broswer using Live Server.

**Controls:**

- To move: WASD
- To shoot: Spacebar (shoots the direction you're facing)
- To skip to boss fight: B
- To restart: R (only once the game is done)

Move your way up and out of the forest, but be sure to avoid rocky obstacles and werewolves!
Use the minimap in the bottom left corner to track the werewolves.
Once you're out of the forest, enter the alpha boss zone and defeat the alpha werewolf!
